# A Thought to Carry — all 28

Plain-English renderings of Tao Te Ching / Zhuangzi passages, one chosen to sit beside each day's theme. Chapter numbers point to the source passage; the wording is mine, so swap freely.

1. **Rest & Peace** — "Return to the root, and the root is stillness. To return to stillness is to return to what does not change." — *Tao Te Ching, 16*

2. **Healing** — "No one can see themselves in running water. Only in water that has come to rest." — *Zhuangzi, 5*

3. **Identity** — "To know others is knowledge. To know yourself is clear sight." — *Tao Te Ching, 33*

4. **Courage** — "The sage meets what cannot be avoided and is not shaken by it. This is the courage of a whole man." — *Zhuangzi, 17*

5. **Wisdom** — "To know that you do not know is best. To not know, and think you know, is the sickness." — *Tao Te Ching, 71*

6. **Joy** — "See how the small fish dart about, free and easy. That is what fishes enjoy." — *Zhuangzi, 17*

7. **His Calling** — "The tree that fills your arms grew from a shoot no thicker than a thread. A journey of a thousand miles begins beneath your feet." — *Tao Te Ching, 64*

8. **Provision** — "To know when you have enough is to be rich." — *Tao Te Ching, 33*

9. **His Mind** — "Thirty spokes meet at one hub. It is the empty space at the centre that makes the wheel of use." — *Tao Te Ching, 11*

10. **Freedom** — "He who is bound by nothing has nowhere he cannot go." — *Zhuangzi, 1*

11. **Fatherhood** — "It gives life and does not possess. It guides and does not rule. This is the deepest kind of care." — *Tao Te Ching, 51*

12. **Relationships** — "Those who are good, I am good to. Those who are not good, I am also good to. In this way goodness is gained." — *Tao Te Ching, 49*

13. **Leadership** — "The best leader is barely known to those he leads. When the work is finished, they say: we did this ourselves." — *Tao Te Ching, 17*

14. **Discipline** — "Meet the hard while it is still easy. Do the great while it is still small." — *Tao Te Ching, 63*

15. **Hope** — "Returning is the movement of the way. Yielding is how it works." — *Tao Te Ching, 40*

16. **Protection** — "He walks through the world and finds no place where harm can enter." — *Tao Te Ching, 50*

17. **Humility** — "The rivers and seas rule the hundred valleys because they know how to lie below them." — *Tao Te Ching, 66*

18. **Strength** — "Nothing in the world is softer than water, yet nothing is better at wearing away what is hard." — *Tao Te Ching, 78*

19. **Patience** — "Who can be still until the muddy water clears? Who can stay at rest until the moment to move arrives?" — *Tao Te Ching, 15*

20. **Gratitude** — "There is no greater misfortune than not knowing what is enough." — *Tao Te Ching, 46*

21. **Creativity** — "The cook's blade has been used nineteen years and is still as sharp as new. He does not cut through; he finds the openings that are already there." — *Zhuangzi, 3*

22. **Integrity** — "In dwelling, keep to the ground. In speaking, keep to the truth. In acting, keep to what is right." — *Tao Te Ching, 8*

23. **Love** — "I have three treasures which I hold and keep. The first of them is compassion." — *Tao Te Ching, 67*

24. **Faith** — "The way is hidden and has no name. Yet it alone nourishes and brings to completion." — *Tao Te Ching, 41*

25. **Legacy** — "What is well planted cannot be pulled up. What is well held cannot slip away. So it continues, generation after generation." — *Tao Te Ching, 54*

26. **Abundance** — "The sage does not store things up. The more he does for others, the more he has for himself." — *Tao Te Ching, 81*

27. **Thanksgiving** — "The earth gives me a body, wears me out with life, eases me with age, and rests me with death. What makes my life good makes my death good also." — *Zhuangzi, 6*

28. **Blessing** — "Deep roots and a firm foundation. This is the way of long life and enduring sight." — *Tao Te Ching, 59*

*(Day 1 now carries both the Rest and Peace prayers, so it keeps the stillness passage from chapter 16; the water passage from chapter 8 that sat with Peace is no longer used.)*
