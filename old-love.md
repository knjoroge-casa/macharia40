# Old Love

Truly. Madly. Deeply.

LEGEND HAS IT THAT THEY WERE NEVER APART FOR MORE THAN A FEW HOURS.

This isn't a countdown. It's a pause.

For the next twenty-eight days, I wanted to give you something that couldn't be wrapped, spent, or eventually forgotten. So instead of a gift you'll open once, I wrote you one you'll open every day.

Each morning, another page unfolds. Another part of your life is covered in prayer. Your rest. Your healing. Your mind. Your work. Your daughters. Our relationship. Your future. The man you are. The man you are still becoming.

After each prayer, you'll find **

A Thought to Carry

**—a small piece of Taoist wisdom that reminded me of you. Not to answer life's questions, but to sit with you as you move through your day.

Some days these words may feel exactly right. Some days they may not. That's okay. Return to them when you need them. They were never written just for these twenty-eight days. They were written for every version of you that comes after.

There is no race to the end. No streaks to keep. No boxes to tick. Just one page each day.

I hope that years from now, this feels less like an app and more like a well-loved notebook you've reached for so many times that it naturally falls open to the pages you needed most.

Thank you for letting me walk beside you, not just in this chapter, but in the story we're still writing together.

~ Wangui waku, always.
